## Implemented producer and smoke

`robo.roundtrip.system_verification` extends `ProposalRecord` and the existing orchestrator ledger/descendant validator. Its native model/data hook measures the actual imported CoACD convex parts at their estimated room pose, closest context pairs, initial contacts/penetration, actual collision world bounds, settling displacement/rotation, and final contacts. Full integration state is restored byte-exact after probing; no favorable post-settle state becomes the rollout reset.

Public TRAIN RGB-D only supplies the local horizontal support proxy. Its plane is the dominant depth-height bin in an annulus around the estimated footprint; it is neither hidden native support geometry nor a general surface-reconstruction guarantee. Pose correction is bounded by 40 mm, changes only world z in a new copied factory artifact, and records invalidation of world collision, native import, task handles, contact lists, GS transform and background completion. Local mesh and collision bytes are unchanged.

Shared action bank: observation-derived support correction and selection of the first predeclared alternative candidate from the frozen pool. Version 1 B4 scheduled support then reselection when checks failed; version 3 uses the frozen failure-class scheduler below. BM executes fixed reselection then support, skipping only formal inapplicability; both have at most two actions and the same final acceptance/selection. V1 has no actions. Finite drifting B3 is eligible for native execution. Frozen candidate pool hashes and capture binding are checked when supplied. Maximum-budget matching is not exact compute matching.
